How to play

  • Each arrow points up, down, left or right.
  • Tap an arrow to remove it - but only if its lane to the board edge is completely clear of other arrows.
  • If something blocks the lane, the arrow shakes and you lose a heart.
  • Clear every arrow to finish the level. You have 3 hearts.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint to highlight an arrow you can remove right now.
  • Packs adds optional extra levels - everything else works offline.
